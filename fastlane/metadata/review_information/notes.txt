Thank you for reviewing Catgrid Collection: Nonogram.

- No account or login is needed. All progress is stored on the device.
- Muffin's School (the first chapter) teaches the rules in 12 short lessons with Muffin, a teacher cat. It can be skipped with "Skip tutorial". No ads are shown during the tutorial.
- Cat breeds unlock in order: the first breed opens after Muffin's School (or right away if it is skipped), and each next breed opens once half of the previous breed's puzzles are solved. The Premium screen can be opened from the home screen at any time, without playing.
- Ads (Google AdMob) appear only between puzzles, after every 2 solved puzzles with at least 90 seconds between ads; never during play. Rewarded ads are optional (extra paw / extra time after losing).
- In the EEA/UK the Google consent form appears on first launch, followed by the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- Catgrid Premium (auto-renewable, monthly or yearly) removes ads and unlocks 9 Golden Puzzles per breed plus Golden Cards. Open it from the home screen banner ("Go Premium"), from Settings → Premium, or from the "Golden Puzzles" teaser at the bottom of a breed's level screen. As a free sample, the 9 Golden Puzzles of British Shorthair unlock for everyone once that breed is finished. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are in Settings → Premium.
- The Golden Card shown in the screenshots is earned by solving a breed's 9 Golden Puzzles (Premium, or the free British Shorthair sample); the screenshot captions say "with Premium".
- Privacy Policy, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Support links are in Settings → About and on the subscription screen.
